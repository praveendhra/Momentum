Support

Need help with Momentum, found a bug, or have an idea? Email momentum@mail.praveendhra.com and you'll get a reply, usually within a couple of days.

Common questions

How do I log something?
Tap an activity on the Today board. Yes/no habits log in one tap; others open a short form.

Can I log a day I missed?
Yes. Tap that day on the heatmap and choose "Log something for this day", or change the date in the log form.

Does my data sync between my phone and the website?
Yes. Sign in with the same account and everything is there.

How do I change units, theme or reminders?
Open Settings. Units, theme, week start, time zone and email reminders are all there.

I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot your password?" and follow the email link.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all your data.
