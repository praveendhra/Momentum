Momentum is a habit tracker that rewards showing up, not perfection.

Most habit apps reset you to zero the day you miss. Momentum counts the days you showed up: every check-in is a block, and you stack them one at a time.

• TODAY BOARD: tick off habits in one tap and see progress toward each daily goal (pages read, glasses of water, minutes meditated).
• YEAR HEATMAP: every day is a block that glows brighter the more you did. Filter by habit or category, switch years, and tap any day to see exactly what you did.
• STREAKS THAT FORGIVE: daily streaks plus weekly streaks ("17 weeks in a row") for habits you do a few times a week, so one missed day never wipes out months of work.
• MILESTONES & LIFETIME TOTALS: 5, 10, 25, 50, 100 sessions and beyond, with fun totals like "16 hours ≈ 8 movies" or "20 books read".
• MOOD CHECK-INS: rate your mood, energy and stress, add a gratitude note, and after two weeks see which habits lift your mood.
• ANY HABIT: reading, meditation, water, sleep, journaling, language practice, coding, music, workouts and runs, or create your own with any emoji, colour, unit and daily goal.
• YOUR WAY: light and dark themes, units, week start, time zone, and optional email reminders plus a Sunday weekly summary.
• SIGN IN WITH APPLE or email. Your habits sync with the web app at momentum.praveendhra.com.

No ads, no tracking, nothing sold. Delete your account and all data any time in Settings.
