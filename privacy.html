Momentum

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Momentum is a personal tracker for workouts, habits, skill practice and mood. It's built and run by Praveendhra Rajkumar. This policy explains what data the app keeps, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: your data is used only to run the app for you. There are no ads, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash). If you sign in with Google on the website, we receive your name and email address from Google.
  • What you log: activities you create, sessions you log (dates, durations, distances, sets, reps, weights, amounts), mood, energy and stress scores, contributing factors and any notes or journal text you write.
  • Settings: theme, units, week start, time zone and email notification choices.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health data from other apps, advertising identifiers or device analytics.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show you your own data on any device.
  • To calculate your stats, streaks, personal records and mood insights (this happens on your device).
  • To send the emails you turn on: a daily reminder and a weekly summary. They're off until you enable them, and you can turn them off any time in Settings.

Where it's stored and who processes it

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in. Database security rules make sure each account can only read and write its own data.
  • Resend delivers notification emails (it receives your email address and the email content).
  • GitHub Pages serves the website.
  • Google handles "Continue with Google" sign-in on the website, if you choose it.

These providers process data only to provide their service to Momentum. All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

Sharing

We don't sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone for advertising or marketing. We would only disclose data if required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete individual entries any time, and you can delete your whole account in the app under Settings → Danger zone → Delete account. That permanently removes your account and everything in it right away.

Children

Momentum is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
